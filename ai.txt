# Halloween - PropFirmDiscount.com

Halloween prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

89 deals from 35 prop firms, newest 2026-10-30.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
